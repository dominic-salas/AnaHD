AnaHD 0.1.2: watch your flight being cleaned.

**New in 0.1.2**

- While a clip is cleaned, the preview shows the frame just cleaned against the
  recording's, updated every second and a half; drag the line across it. If a setting
  looks wrong in motion, Stop is right under it.
- Before the first frame, the clip's card says what it is doing (counting frames,
  measuring, loading the models).
- The header and the tray say when the whole batch should be done, from the speed
  measured on your GPU.
- The cleaned video is exactly the same as 0.1.1's.

AnaHD makes clean HD video from analog FPV goggle DVR recordings, offline, on an NVIDIA
GPU.

**Install:** download `AnaHD-0.1.2-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user
(no admin rights) with everything it needs (FFmpeg, PyTorch's CUDA libraries, the model);
nothing else is downloaded. Uninstall from the Start menu or Windows' Apps list.

**Needs:** Windows 10/11 64-bit; an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 16 or RTX 20 series card or newer
(RTX 30, 40 and 50 included) with a current driver (CUDA 12.8; only an RTX 4070 Ti has been
tested; GTX 10 series and older are not supported); about 7 GB of free memory (RAM plus page file) while
a clip is cleaned; about 5 GB of disk.

### Please read: built for one pilot's gear

AnaHD is a personal tool, shared as is. Its **Learned** cleaning (the default) was trained
on one camera (BetaFPV C03), one analog link and one goggle DVR (1280x720, 60 fps, 4:3
picture between black bars). On other cameras, VTXs or DVRs it runs, but the look may be
off: over-smoothed, invented texture, shifted colour. **Try it on a short clip first** (cut
the rest) and compare it with the **Classic** cleaning (bottom of the window), which is
hand-made and behaves the same on any analog recording. Keep whichever looks better.

- OSD protection works with any burned-in OSD (it finds overlay that stays put).
- SD DVRs (720x480, 720x576 at 50 fps, 640x480 MJPEG) come out at the right 4:3 shape.
- Clips **without an OSD** are taken for digital recordings and left out of cleaning. If
  you fly analog without OSD, press **Include anyway** on them.

### What it does

- Repairs breakup bands, colour snow and lost colour from the neighbouring frames;
  cleans the noise; upscales to 1440x1080 (4:3) or 1920x1080 (16:9) at the recording's
  frame rate; keeps the OSD sharp.
- Desktop app: preview, cut markers (exact times, half-second steps), one file per clip or
  one per kept part, before/after comparison of the results. Keeps working in the tray
  when you close the window; never quits by itself.
- Remaster settings: a lens colour fix and a film-style look (on by default), colour
  sliders, and optional stabilisation, sharpening, SuperView and motion blur, with a
  before/after preview of the paused frame and each effect's speed cost.
- 21.9 fps on an RTX 4070 Ti with the default settings: a 3-minute flight in about 8.4
  minutes, start-up and encoding included.
- Outputs `AnaHD_<name>.mp4`; never overwrites anything and never changes your recordings.

### Known limitations

- Very fast flips (around 720°/s): the motion is kept exactly, but edges smear and fine
  texture flattens, slightly worse than a plain upscale.
- Faint dark halos along hard edges with the Learned cleaning.
- Breakup longer than 3 frames is not rebuilt.
