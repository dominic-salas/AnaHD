AnaHD 0.2.0: 1440p output, TensorRT, HDZero recordings upscaled without analog cleaning.

**New in 0.2.0**

- **1440p output.** 4:3 pictures come out at 1920x1440 and 16:9 pictures at 2560x1440
  (a 16:9 camera recorded with side blanking now gets the full 16:9 shape). The Learned
  cleaning's own 2x detail is kept instead of being shrunk to 1080 lines. Classic uses the
  SPAN 2x upscaler by default.
- **TensorRT.** When NVIDIA's free TensorRT is present, clips of 10 seconds or more run
  faster. It is too big for the installer (about 2.2 GB), so the app offers a
  **Download TensorRT** button; without it everything still works. The first clean of a new
  picture size says "Preparing TensorRT for this picture size (first time only)".
- **HDZero recordings are upscaled without analog cleaning.** HDZero at 90 fps or
  1920x1080 is recognised from the file; HDZero at 1280x720 and 60 fps (for example an
  HDZero Lux) is recognised from the picture itself. In testing on one pilot's recordings,
  no analog recording was taken for HDZero and 39 of 45 HDZero recordings were found; the
  ones missed (dark or blurry clips) are cleaned as analog, as before. **Clean as analog**
  overrides it.
- **RaceCam R1 Mini hint.** For 4:3 recordings at 50 fps the app suggests trying Classic,
  which keeps fine detail such as bare branches better on that camera.

AnaHD makes clean HD video from analog FPV goggle DVR recordings, offline, on an NVIDIA
GPU.

**Install:** download `AnaHD-0.2.0-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user
(no admin rights) with everything it needs (FFmpeg, PyTorch's CUDA libraries, the model);
nothing else is needed (TensorRT is an optional download from inside the app). Uninstall from the Start menu or Windows' Apps list.

**Needs:** Windows 10/11 64-bit; an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 16 or RTX 20 series card or newer
(RTX 30, 40 and 50 included) with a current driver (CUDA 12.8; only an RTX 4070 Ti has been
tested; GTX 10 series and older are not supported); about 7 GB of free memory (RAM plus page file) while
a clip is cleaned; about 5 GB of disk.

### Please read: built for one pilot's gear

AnaHD is a personal tool, shared as is. Its **Learned** cleaning (the default) was trained
on one camera (BetaFPV C03), one analog link and one goggle DVR (1280x720, 60 fps, 4:3
picture between black bars). On other cameras, VTXs or DVRs it runs, but the look may be
off: over-smoothed, invented texture, shifted colour. **Try it on a short clip first** (cut
the rest) and compare it with the **Classic** cleaning (bottom of the window), which is
hand-made and behaves the same on any analog recording. Keep whichever looks better.

- OSD protection works with any burned-in OSD drawn with an outlined font (it finds the OSD by its strokes).
- SD DVRs (720x480, 720x576 at 50 fps, 640x480 MJPEG) come out at the right 4:3 shape.
- Clips **without an OSD** that are not recognised as HDZero are left out of cleaning. If
  you fly analog without OSD, press **Include anyway** on them.

### What it does

- Repairs breakup bands, colour snow and lost colour from the neighbouring frames;
  cleans the noise; upscales to 1920x1440 (4:3) or 2560x1440 (16:9) at the recording's
  frame rate; keeps the OSD sharp.
- Desktop app: preview, cut markers (exact times, half-second steps), one file per clip or
  one per kept part, before/after comparison of the results. Keeps working in the tray
  when you close the window; never quits by itself.
- Remaster settings: a lens colour fix and a film-style look (on by default), colour
  sliders, and optional stabilisation, sharpening, SuperView and motion blur, with a
  before/after preview of the paused frame and each effect's speed cost.
- About 18 fps on an RTX 4070 Ti with the default settings and a 4:3 picture: a 3-minute
  flight in about 10 minutes (16:9 at 2560x1440: about 12 fps, 15 minutes).
- Outputs `AnaHD_<name>.mp4`; never overwrites anything and never changes your recordings.

### Known limitations

- Very fast flips (around 720°/s): the motion is kept exactly, but edges smear and fine
  texture flattens, slightly worse than a plain upscale.
- Faint dark halos along hard edges with the Learned cleaning.
- Breakup longer than 3 frames is not rebuilt.
- Some cameras (seen on a RaceCam R1 Mini) have coarse colour blotches that both cleanings
  keep; the default look makes them more visible.
