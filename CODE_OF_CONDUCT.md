# Code of conduct

AnaHD's issues, pull requests and discussions are for pilots and developers of any
background and experience. Everyone taking part, maintainers included, is expected to:

- be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to FPV, video or code;
- discuss the footage and the code, not the person; disagree with reasons;
- accept that a "no" or "not now" on a feature is about the project's scope;
- respect privacy: don't post other people's footage, locations or personal details
  without their consent.

Not accepted, anywhere in the project:

- harassment, insults, threats, or attacks on someone's identity or background;
- sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention;
- trolling, spam, or deliberately derailing a discussion;
- publishing someone's private information.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, report it privately through the repository's
**Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**). That form reaches only the maintainer;
say that it is a conduct report. Reports are read in confidence, and the person reporting
is not identified to the person reported without consent.

## What happens

The maintainer decides what to do, in proportion to what happened: a private word, a
public note, hiding or deleting comments, or a temporary or permanent block from the
project. Behaviour outside the project that targets its members can count too.

This applies in all of AnaHD's spaces on GitHub, and when someone represents the project
elsewhere.
